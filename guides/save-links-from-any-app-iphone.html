How to save links from any app on iPhone

By Devon, maker of Bulletn

To save a link from almost any iPhone app, tap Share and pick the app where you want to keep it. The Share sheet works the same basic way in most apps, so one habit covers most of your saving. The real choice is where the link goes. Here are the common places, and when each one makes sense.

Start with the Share button

Most apps that show posts, videos, articles or products have a Share button. It opens a list of apps that can receive the link.

  1. Open the post, video or page.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. If the app shows its own share panel first, look for More or Share to. That opens the iPhone share sheet.
  4. Pick the app you want.

If an app is missing from the row, scroll to the end of the row and tap More to find it.

No Share button? Look for a way to copy the link. You can paste it wherever you keep your links.

Places to keep links

Safari bookmarks and Reading List

Safari has bookmarks and a Reading List. Both are good for web pages. Use bookmarks for sites you visit often and Reading List for articles you plan to read. They are less handy for a mix of videos and posts from other apps.

The Notes app

A note can hold any link, and you can search your notes. It is free and already on your iPhone. The cost is upkeep. A note with 60 links turns into a wall of text, and you have to label and sort each one.

A message to yourself

Texting or emailing yourself a link is quick. But it gets mixed with everything else in your messages, and there are no folders.

Saved lists inside each app

TikTok has Favorites. Instagram and Facebook each have a Saved section. YouTube has playlists. These work well inside their own app. The trouble starts when you save from several, because each list only knows about itself.

An app made for saved links

An organizer app takes links from all of these and keeps them together. This is the best fit if you save from three or four apps and keep losing things.

What makes a link easy to find later

  • One place. If you have to guess which app has it, you will give up.
  • A clear title or note. Your own words are the ones you will search for.
  • A folder. Even a rough one, like Recipes or Trips, cuts the list down.
  • A reason to come back. A reminder turns a saved link into a plan.
