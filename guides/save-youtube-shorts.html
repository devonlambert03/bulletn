How to save YouTube Shorts and keep them organized

By Devon, maker of Bulletn

To save a YouTube Short, add it to a YouTube playlist, or tap Share and send the link to an app that keeps your saves in folders. Playlists are fine for a few Shorts. Folders you can search are better once you save a lot. Here is how to do both.

Save Shorts inside YouTube

YouTube has playlists, and you can add a Short to one. A playlist called "Dinner ideas" or "Guitar practice" keeps similar videos together. Your playlists belong to your YouTube account, so you can open them anywhere you sign in.

Make the playlists before you need them. If the right one already exists, saving takes a moment. If it does not, most people skip it.

A like is not a filing system

Many people tap like on a Short and hope to find it again. Liking does add the video to your liked videos. But that list covers every topic at once. A funny dog can end up next to the bread recipe you wanted.

Watch history has the same problem. It remembers what you watched, not what you wanted to keep.

If you want to use a Short later, save it on purpose.

Why Shorts are hard to find again

  • They go by fast. You watch many in a row. One swipe and it is gone.
  • Titles are often vague. "This changed my mornings" could be about anything.
  • They look alike. A row of small thumbnails does not tell you much.
  • The same topics live elsewhere. You may have a pasta Short on YouTube, a pasta reel on Instagram and a pasta video on TikTok.

How to keep Shorts organized

  • Save by use. "Cook this week" is more helpful than "Food."
  • Start with four or five folders. Add another when one gets too full.
  • Add a few words of your own. A note like "20 minute leg day, no weights" makes the video easy to find.
  • Remove what you tried. Keep the good ones and let the rest go.

Save YouTube Shorts to Bulletn

Bulletn is an iPhone app that keeps links from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and any app with a Share button in one place. To save a Short:

  1. On the Short, tap Share.
  2. Open the iPhone share sheet. Some apps label this More or Share to.
  3. Pick Bulletn.
  4. Choose a folder or leave it on AI sorting, then tap Save.

The first time, you may need to scroll to the end of the row of apps and tap More to find Bulletn.

Sorting

AI sorting reads the title, caption and link and puts the Short in the folder that fits. When nothing fits, it suggests a new folder. When it is not sure, the save waits in Unsorted with a one tap suggestion. You can always move a save yourself.

That also means a pasta Short can sit in the same Recipes folder as the pasta reel and the pasta TikTok. You do not have to remember which app it came from.

Finding

Search by title, note or folder across all your saves. Folders show your latest saves as photo tiles, and you can add a photo cover so each one is easy to spot.

Coming back

Add a reminder as you save. If you want to try a stretch on Saturday, the Short comes back on Saturday.

Sharing

You can share a whole folder with one link. Your friend opens it in a browser and sees the saves, even without Bulletn.

Good to know

Bulletn saves the link to a Short. If the creator takes the Short down, the link will not bring it back. A YouTube playlist has the same limit.
