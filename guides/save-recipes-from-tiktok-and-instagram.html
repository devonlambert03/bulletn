How to save recipes from TikTok and Instagram in one place

By Devon, maker of Bulletn

You saw a great recipe on TikTok, another on Instagram, and a third on YouTube. At 5 pm you cannot find any of them. Here are the common ways to keep recipe videos together, and what each one costs you.

Why recipes get lost

Each app has its own saved list. TikTok has Favorites. Instagram has Saved. YouTube has Watch Later and playlists. A recipe you saved is only easy to find if you remember which app it came from.

Recipe videos are also hard to scan. The thumbnail is often a person talking, not the dish. And the actual recipe may be in the caption, the comments or a link in the creator's profile.

Option 1: Save inside each app

This is the default, and it works if you only save a few recipes.

  • TikTok: tap the bookmark on the right side of the video. Find it later in your Profile, under the bookmark tab.
  • Instagram: tap the bookmark under the post or reel. Find it in your Profile, then the menu, then Saved.
  • YouTube: tap Save under the video and choose Watch Later or a playlist.

Make a Recipes collection or playlist in each app so they do not mix with everything else. The downside: three apps, three lists, three places to check.

Option 2: Screenshots

Many people screenshot the ingredient list. It is fast and works offline. But screenshots get buried in your camera roll, they lose the link back to the video, and a screenshot cannot show you the technique.

Option 3: A note full of links

Copy each link into one note called Recipes. Everything is in one place and you can search the note. The cost is effort: you copy, switch apps, paste, and maybe type a title so you know what the link is. Most people stop after a week.

Tips that work with any method

  • Save the moment you see it. You will not find it again by scrolling.
  • Check the caption first. If the full recipe is there, you have what you need. If it says "recipe in bio," save that link too.
  • Split by when you will cook it. A "This week" folder next to a general Recipes folder makes meal planning faster.
  • Try one, then decide. Delete recipes that did not work out so your list stays worth opening.

How Bulletn keeps recipes in one place

Bulletn is an iPhone app that collects saves from every app and sorts them for you. It works with TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and any app with a Share button.

  1. On a recipe video, tap Share.
  2. Open the iPhone share sheet. Some apps label this More or Share to.
  3. Pick Bulletn.

AI Auto-sort files the save into your Recipes folder, next to recipes from every other app. If Bulletn is not sure where something goes, it waits in Unsorted with one suggested folder. One tap files it.

At dinner time, open Recipes or search across all your saves. Planning to make something on the weekend? Set a reminder on it. Add a photo cover to the Recipes folder so it is easy to spot.
