How to save Facebook videos and find them later

By Devon, maker of Bulletn

To save a Facebook video, use Facebook's own save option and look for it later in the Saved section. If you want it next to your saves from other apps, tap Share and send the link to an organizer app. Here is how both ways work.

Save it inside Facebook

Facebook has a Saved section. It holds videos, posts and links you chose to keep. You can also group saved items into collections, which work like folders.

This is the simplest option. Nothing to install, and it is tied to your Facebook account. If you only save from Facebook, start here.

One thing to know: saving keeps a pointer to the video. It does not put a copy on your phone. If the person who posted it deletes it or changes who can see it, your save may stop working.

Why saved Facebook videos get lost

  • Everything lands together. A recipe, a funny clip and a news story can all end up in one list.
  • Sorting is up to you. Collections help, but only if you file each save yourself.
  • You forget why you saved it. A video titled "Wait for it" tells you nothing a month later.
  • It is one app out of many. Your TikTok, Instagram and YouTube saves are in other places.

Habits that help

These work no matter where you save.

  • Save it right away. A feed moves on. You may not see that video again.
  • Use a few clear folders. Recipes, Workouts, Home and Kids cover a lot.
  • Write down why. "Dinner for Mom's birthday" is easier to find than the title of the video.
  • Clear out what you used. A short list gets opened. A long one gets ignored.

Save Facebook videos to Bulletn

Bulletn is an iPhone app that keeps saves from every app in one place. It saves the link to the video. To save one:

  1. On the video, tap Share.
  2. Open the iPhone share sheet. Some apps label this More or Share to.
  3. Pick Bulletn.
  4. Pick a folder yourself, or let Bulletn sort it for you. Then tap Save.

If Bulletn is not in the row of apps the first time, scroll to the end of the row and tap More to find it.

It sorts the save

With AI sorting on, Bulletn reads the title, caption and link and puts the save in the folder that fits. A chicken dinner goes to Recipes. A stretching video goes to Fitness. AI sorting asks for your consent first, and you can turn it off in Settings.

If Bulletn is not sure, the save waits in Unsorted with one suggested folder. Tap the suggestion and it moves there. You can also move any save yourself.

You can find it again

Search by title, note or folder. The search covers all your saves, so it does not matter if the video came from Facebook or somewhere else. Folders have photo covers, and you can pin the ones you use most.

Add a note as you save, like "use less salt" or "for the back porch." Notes are searchable, so even a video with a useless title can be found.

It can come back to you

Set a reminder on a save. That helps with a recipe for Sunday or a workout for Monday morning.

What about videos you already saved?

Your older saves stay in Facebook's Saved section. You do not need to move them all. Start with new saves, and bring over an old one when you want it: open the video, tap Share and pick Bulletn.

Which one should you use?

Use Facebook's Saved section if Facebook is the only place you save from. Use Bulletn if your saves are spread over several apps, or if you never get around to sorting them.
