How to find your saved Instagram posts and reels

By Devon, maker of Bulletn

You tapped the bookmark on a reel weeks ago. Now you want it back. Here is where Instagram keeps your saves, and how to make them easier to find next time.

Where your saved posts live

Instagram keeps everything you save in one private list called Saved. Only you can see it. On iPhone:

  1. Open Instagram and tap your profile picture in the bottom right corner.
  2. Tap the menu button (three lines) in the top right.
  3. Tap Saved.

You will see All posts, which holds every post and reel you have saved, plus any collections you made. Newer saves show first.

On a computer, open instagram.com, go to your profile, and look for the Saved tab.

How saving works

Under a photo post, the bookmark icon sits on the right. On a reel, look for the same bookmark, or tap the three dots and choose Save. When the bookmark turns solid, the post is in your Saved list.

Saving does not download anything. It keeps a pointer to the post. If the creator deletes it or makes their account private, it can drop out of your list.

Use collections to stay sorted

A long Saved list is hard to scroll. Collections help. They work like folders inside Saved.

  1. Open Saved.
  2. Tap the plus button to make a new collection and give it a name, like Dinner Ideas or Home Workouts.
  3. Pick the saved posts you want to add.

You can also file a post as you save it. After you tap the bookmark, Instagram shows a small prompt to save to a collection. Tap it and pick one.

A few tips that keep collections useful:

  • Name them by what you will do, not by topic. "Cook this week" gets opened. "Food" becomes a second junk drawer.
  • Keep the number small. Five clear collections beat twenty vague ones.
  • Clean out once a month. Unsave what you already tried or no longer want.

Why saved posts are still hard to find

Collections help, but a few problems remain.

  • You have to sort by hand. Every save needs a second tap to land in a collection. Most people skip it, so All posts keeps growing.
  • Finding one post means scrolling. You are looking at a grid of thumbnails and trying to remember what the one you want looked like.
  • It only holds Instagram. The pasta recipe you saved on TikTok and the workout you saved on YouTube live in other apps, with their own lists.

If you mostly save from Instagram and only save a few things a week, collections may be all you need. If you save from several apps, or your Saved list has hundreds of posts, a separate place for saves can help.

How Bulletn helps

Bulletn is an iPhone app that keeps saves from every app in one place and sorts them for you.

  1. On a post or reel, tap the share button (the paper plane).
  2. Open the iPhone share sheet. In Instagram this may be labeled Share to or More.
  3. Pick Bulletn. The link is saved.

The first time, Bulletn may not be in the row of apps. Scroll to the end of the row and tap More to find it.

AI Auto-sort then files the save into the right folder, like Recipes, Fitness or Travel. If it is not sure, the link waits in Unsorted with one suggested folder. One tap files it.

When you need it again, search across all your saves instead of scrolling a grid. You can also set a reminder on a save, so the reel you meant to try comes back to you.
