How to organize your saved TikToks

By Devon, maker of Bulletn

Saving a TikTok takes one tap. Finding it again three weeks later is the hard part. Here is how TikTok's Favorites work, how to sort them, and when to use something else.

Where saved TikToks go

TikTok calls saved videos Favorites. To see them:

  1. Open TikTok and tap Profile in the bottom right.
  2. Tap the bookmark tab under your profile details.

Your Favorites are split into sections such as videos, sounds and collections. Saved videos are listed with the newest first.

Favorites are not the same as Likes. Liking a video (the heart) adds it to your Liked tab. Saving it (the bookmark) adds it to Favorites. If you want to find a video again, save it. Likes pile up fast and are hard to dig through.

How to save a TikTok

While a video plays, tap the bookmark icon in the column on the right side of the screen. It turns yellow when the video is saved. You can also tap Share and look for Add to Favorites.

Saving keeps a link to the video. It does not download it. If the creator removes the video, it can disappear from your Favorites.

Sort saves into collections

Collections are folders inside Favorites. To make one:

  1. Go to your Favorites.
  2. Open the collections section and tap to create a new collection.
  3. Name it and choose the saved videos to add.

Right after you tap the bookmark, TikTok may also show a short prompt to add the video to a collection. Tapping it is the fastest way to file as you go.

Habits that keep it organized

  • Pick a few clear folders. Recipes, Workouts, Kids, Travel and Buy Later cover most saves. Add more only when one gets crowded.
  • File at save time. Sorting a backlog of 400 videos is a chore nobody finishes. One extra tap now saves an hour later.
  • Name folders for an action. "Try this week" tells you what to do. "Misc" does not.
  • Prune often. Unsave videos you already used. A shorter list is a list you will actually open.

Where TikTok's tools fall short

TikTok's collections work well if you remember to use them. Many people do not. A few other limits:

  • Sorting is manual. Every video needs you to choose a collection.
  • Finding one video means scrolling. You flip through thumbnails hoping to spot the one you want.
  • It is TikTok only. The workout you saved on Instagram and the recipe you saved on YouTube are somewhere else.

If TikTok is the only app you save from, collections plus the habits above will get you far. If your saves are spread across several apps, it helps to keep them in one place.

How Bulletn organizes saved TikToks

Bulletn is an iPhone app that sorts your saves for you. To save a TikTok to it:

  1. On the video, tap Share.
  2. In TikTok's share panel, tap More to open the iPhone share sheet.
  3. Pick Bulletn. The link is saved.

If Bulletn is not in the row of apps the first time, scroll to the end of the row and tap More to find it.

AI Auto-sort files each save into the right folder, like Recipes, Fitness or Kid Activities. Links it is not sure about wait in Unsorted with one suggested folder, and one tap files them.

Search across all your saves, not just TikTok. Set a reminder on a save you want to come back to. If you file something in the wrong place, undo it, or select several saves and move them at once.
