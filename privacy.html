Privacy Policy

Bulletn: Saved & Sorted. Effective November 2026.

The short version

Your saved links stay on your iPhone. Bulletn has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. If AI sorting is on, each new link is sent once to be sorted and is not stored.

What stays on your iPhone

Your saved links, titles, notes, folders and preview images are stored only on your device. They are not synced to iCloud or to any server by Bulletn.

AI sorting

When AI sorting is on, Bulletn sends a new save's link, title and note, together with the names of your folders, to Bulletn's server. The server asks Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, which folder fits, and sends the answer back to the app. Bulletn's server does not store or log the link, title, note, folder names or the answer.

Anthropic processes this request under its commercial terms and states that it does not use API data to train its models. Anthropic may keep requests for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring.

To protect the service from abuse, Bulletn's server keeps an anonymous device key created by Apple's App Attest, a daily count of sorts per device and a monthly total of AI costs. None of these identify you.

You can turn AI sorting off at any time in Bulletn's Settings, and you can choose Unsorted on the share screen to keep a single link from being sorted.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Bulletn never sees your payment details.

Children

Bulletn is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes
